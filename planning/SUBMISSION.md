**Project name:** Sightline

**Tagline:** Self-configuring video safety agents

**Description (about 100 words):**
Most video AI needs someone to tell it what to look for. Sightline looks first. Pointed at a camera, it classifies the environment from VAST's index and YOLO detections, generates its own monitoring objectives and rules, notices what the existing descriptions don't capture, and writes its own Cosmos analysis prompt. It then monitors the archive, raises candidate person–vehicle incidents without anyone typing a search, and investigates each one with before/after context, other camera angles, a Cosmos second look and a W&B verdict that must cite real footage. Same engine, different camera: warehouse forklifts and street crossings configured themselves differently.

**Tools used:** VAST Data AI OS (S3, DataEngine, VastDB, Video Search & Summary APIs, re-ingest), NVIDIA Cosmos Reason, NVIDIA Cosmos Embed, YOLO11, CoreWeave GPUs, Weights & Biases Inference (Nemotron 3 Ultra, Llama 3.3 70B), Cursor, FastAPI, Kubernetes.
